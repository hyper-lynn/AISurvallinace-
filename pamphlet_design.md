### 📌 INSIDE PANEL 1: Core Technologies (အဓိက နည်းပညာများ)

#### 1. Human Motion Detection (လူလှုပ်ရှားမှု စောင့်ကြည့်ခြင်း)
* Analyzes live video streams from existing IP/RTSP cameras.
* Instantly detects movement in restricted zones, storage rooms, and off-hour periods.

#### 2. Face Recognition & Verification (မျက်နှာ ခွဲခြားစစ်ဆေးခြင်း)
* Compares facial encodings against stored employee profiles in real time.
* Differentiates Authorized Personnel from Unknown / Unauthorized Strangers in seconds.

---

### 📌 INSIDE PANEL 2: Media Alert Integration (တိုက်ရိုက် သတိပေးစနစ်)

#### 📸 Rich Media Notifications (ဓာတ်ပုံ/ဗီဒီယိုပါ သတိပေးချက်များ)
* Does not rely on generic text alarms.
* Sends **High-Resolution Photo Snapshots** and **Short Video Clips** directly to target channels.

#### 📲 Multi-Channel Dispatching
* **Telegram Bot API:** Instant delivery to security personnel chats and group channels.
* **Mobile / Desktop Apps:** Real-time push alerts with event timestamp and camera location details.

---

### 📌 INSIDE PANEL 3: Key Security Benefits (အဓိက အကျိုးကျေးဇူးများ)

1. **24/7 Uninterrupted Coverage:** Eliminates security guard fatigue and monitoring oversights.
2. **Proactive Incident Deterrence:** Enables instant response before damage or theft occurs.
3. **Cost Efficiency:** Integrates directly into existing CCTV infrastructure without replacing hardware.
4. **Verifiable Audit Trails:** Maintains digital logs of every detection event for reporting and investigation.
